My Travel Story

Last spring I drove out to the Caney Fork River for my first real fly fishing trip. The water was cold and fast, and I spent the first hour tangling my line in every tree on the bank. By sunset I finally landed a rainbow trout, and I was hooked for good.

"The best trips are the ones where something goes wrong and you figure it out anyway."
