Packing Checklist

Here is what I bring on a weekend fishing trip.

  • Fly rod, reel, and a box of flies
  • Waders and a rain jacket
  • Fishing license
  • Snacks, water, and a first aid kit

Travel Terms

Day trip
A trip where you leave and come home on the same day.
Catch and release
Letting a fish go back into the water after you catch it.
