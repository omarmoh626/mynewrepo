Notes from Kyoto

Early morning in Kyoto is calm and cool. The bamboo grove at Arashiyama sways overhead, and the only sound is the wind moving through the stalks.

Before the tour buses show up, the stone paths are almost empty. It feels like you have the whole forest to yourself for a little while.

By mid-morning the crowds roll in — so setting an early alarm is worth it.
